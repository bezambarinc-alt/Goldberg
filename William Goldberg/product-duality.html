The Duality

Two forces. One truth.

A 10.02-carat Ashoka®-cut sapphire and a 9.13-carat Ashoka®-cut diamond, set in a bypass formation that mirrors the ancient principle of opposing forces in perfect harmony. The deep, saturated blue of the sapphire meets the pure, ice-white brilliance of the diamond — each stone reflecting what the other is not.

The sapphire band is lined with invisible-set sapphires, extending the depth of color into the architecture of the ring itself. The diamond sits opposite, unadorned, letting the stone speak in its own language of light.

This is not a ring of compromise. It is a ring of completion — two stones of nearly equal magnitude, neither yielding to the other, bound in platinum and existing only once.
